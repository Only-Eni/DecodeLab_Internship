## 📅 Weekly Project Logs

### Week 1: Data Cleaning and Preparation
**Status:** Completed
**Tools Used:** Excel

* **Objective:** To audit, clean, and standardize the raw dataset, ensuring high data integrity before any analysis or visualization takes place.
* **Analytical Decisions:** * Verified 100% data integrity across all structural columns. 
  * Audited duplicate `Customer_ID` instances, confirming them as valid repeat transactions to preserve statistical power.
  * Validated that temporal data complies with ISO 8601 and categorical variables strictly adhere to standard naming conventions.
 
### Week 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Business Logic
**Status:** Completed **Tools Used:** Excel (Pivot Tables, Descriptive Statistics)

* **Objective:** To uncover the underlying shape and distribution of e-commerce transactions, isolate structural anomalies, and identify behavioral drivers of high-value revenue.
* **Analytical Decisions:** * Engineered an `Abandoned_Items` metric to quantify frontend checkout friction and calculate lost potential revenue.
  * Deployed the Interquartile Range (IQR) method to isolate mathematical outliers, successfully identifying a VIP bulk-buyer segment.
  * Executed Min/Max pivot aggregations to mathematically prove a critical lack of SKU-level granularity within broad product categories.
